Tsumu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-06 ・ Effective: 2026-06-06

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how Tsumu (the "App") handles information about its users (the "User"). The App is developed and operated by an individual ("we" / "us").

The Japanese version of this Policy is the authoritative original. In the event of any conflict between the Japanese and English versions, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Stored On Your Device

  • Messages you enter, AI replies generated by the App, and the derived "Memories" and "Knowledge" entries. These are stored only in a local SQLite database on your device. We do not store them on our servers.
  • Images you optionally attach to a record. These are stored in the local SQLite database on your device and are not persisted on our servers.
  • App preferences such as theme mode and onboarding-completed flag.

1.2 Information Sent To Our Server For AI Response Generation

When you interact with the App, the following information is transmitted over the Internet to our stateless server (running on Cloudflare Workers) for the sole purpose of generating AI responses:

  • Capture operations: the message you submit, the most recent six (6) turns of conversation history, and any images you optionally attach (converted to compressed JPEG on your device, up to four (4)).
  • Recall operations: your question, the most recent six (6) turns of conversation history, and up to twenty (20) previously saved memory summaries.

Our server forwards the received information to the OpenAI API (a GPT-family large language model) to obtain a response, then returns the response to your device. The server does not persist received content.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Account information (the App has no sign-up): name, address, phone number, date of birth, gender, email address.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / Advertising ID).
  • Location, contacts, microphone, or camera access. (The photo library is accessed only when you choose to attach an image to a record, and only the image you select.)
  • Crash reports or analytics SDKs (Firebase / Crashlytics / Google Analytics, etc.).

1.4 Purchase and Subscription Information

If you purchase the "Tsumu Pro" paid plan, the payment itself is processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play. To manage your purchase status (whether the subscription is active, whether a trial has been used), we send the purchase receipt, the store-issued purchase identifier, and an anonymous in-app user ID generated by the App (a per-device identifier that is not linked to your name or other identifying information) to our processor RevenueCat (see §3.1). Neither we nor RevenueCat collect or retain your credit card number or other payment details.

2. How We Use Information

  • Information sent under §1.2 is used solely to generate the AI response shown to you.
  • We do not use any information for machine-learning training, advertising, profiling, or any other secondary purpose.

Legal basis (GDPR): processing under §1.2 is necessary for the performance of the service requested by the User (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

3. Third-Party Processors and International Transfers

3.1 Processors

We engage the following third parties as processors. They act on our behalf under contract and do not receive your information as independent recipients.

  • OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States)
    • Purpose: AI response generation.
    • Per OpenAI's API terms, content sent through the API is not used by OpenAI to train its models by default. We rely on this default behavior.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States)
    • Purpose: hosting our stateless server (Cloudflare Workers).
    • Cloudflare does not persist conversation content on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States)
    • Purpose: managing paid-plan (subscription) purchase status and entitlements.
    • The information sent is limited to the purchase receipt, the store purchase identifier, and an anonymous in-app user ID; it does not include conversation content or identifying information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third party (advertisers, data brokers, or external companies).

3.2 International Transfers

The processors above are located in the United States. Their respective privacy policies describe the safeguards they apply to personal information. Transfers from the EEA / UK to the United States rely on the processors' standard data-protection commitments (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms maintained by those processors).

4. Storage Locations and Retention

4.1 On-Device Data

  • Memories and conversation history are stored only on your device.
  • You can delete them at any time via the App's "Delete All" function in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.
  • We do not unilaterally set a retention period.

4.2 Server Side

  • Our server does not persist conversation content. Received content is discarded immediately after the response is returned.
  • To prevent abuse, we keep short-lived rate-limit counters per authentication token in Cloudflare Durable Objects. These counters do not contain conversation content.

5. Security

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic between your device and our server, and between our server and OpenAI, uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • On-device protection: memories and conversation history are stored in a local SQLite database. Their protection relies on the operating-system disk encryption (iOS File Protection / Android File-Based Encryption). We have not implemented app-level encryption (such as SQLCipher) at this time. We strongly recommend enabling a passcode / biometric lock and keeping the OS up to date.
  • Access control: our server requires an authentication token; unauthenticated requests are rejected with HTTP 401.
  • Data minimization on the server: by not persisting content, we minimize the risk of server-side data leaks.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your information:

  • Access: you can view all data stored on your device via the App's memory list and settings screens.
  • Deletion: you can erase all on-device memories and conversation history via "Settings → Delete All." Because we do not retain data on our servers, no separate server-side deletion request is required.
  • Correction: you can correct on-device memories via the App's edit features (where available) or by deleting and re-entering them.
  • Complaints: contact us using the address in §8 with any complaint regarding this Policy or our handling of your information.
  • Regulatory complaints: EU / UK users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority; Japan users may contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.

Do-not-sell declaration (CCPA): we do not sell the personal information of any User, including California residents.

  • For material changes (such as new categories of collected information, new purposes, new processors, or weakening of security measures), we may provide additional notice through the App or store listings.
