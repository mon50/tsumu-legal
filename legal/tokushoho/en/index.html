Additional Charges

Internet and mobile data charges required to use and download the App are the responsibility of the user.

Payment

Payment Method

Payment is made through the in-app purchase systems provided by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not collect or retain users' credit card or other payment details.

Payment Timing

You are charged at the time of purchase via the relevant store. Unless cancelled, subscriptions renew automatically on the renewal date defined by each store and are charged at the same amount. Where a free trial applies, you are charged at the end of the trial period.

Delivery / Service Availability

Upon completion of payment, the paid plan (Pro features) is activated and becomes available immediately.

Cancellation, Returns, and Refunds

  • Subscriptions can be stopped at any time from each store's account settings (App Store: "Subscriptions" / Google Play: "Subscriptions"). Cancelling before the renewal date stops the next automatic renewal and charge.
  • After cancellation, Pro features remain available until the end of the period already paid for. No pro-rata refunds are provided for cancellation mid-period as a rule.
  • Due to the nature of digital content (services), cancellations or returns at the user's own convenience after purchase cannot be accepted. Whether a refund is possible and how to request it are governed by Apple's or Google's refund policies.

Operating Environment

The operating environment (supported OS versions, etc.) follows the descriptions on the respective App Store and Google Play listing pages.
